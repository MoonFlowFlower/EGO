# EGO-V2-TRANSFER-MECHANISM-001M

## Authority and bounded objective

- Base HEAD: `b94c61b6f74be9f2f86a67e74437e0850d82be39`.
- Branch: `codex/ego-v2-transfer-mechanism-001m`.
- Runtime authority remains `local_explicit_v2_only`; default product remains the
  default-off, within-world public Bayesian posterior from 001L.
- This successor is dev-only. It does not consume the 001L product
  qualification or the original 001J heldout, and it does not modify or rerun
  any 001L P/R artifact.
- Local commit only. Push, tag, remote anchor, LLM, network, autostart and
  background dispatch are forbidden.

The objective is not to tune transfer gain positive. It is to identify which
public-history-derived information, if any, is reusable under an anonymous
cross-world token permutation while retaining fast within-world posterior
learning.

## Three mechanism candidates and one cross-cutting diagnostic

1. `M1_PERMUTATION_INVARIANT_GRAMMAR`: transfer only the public-history-derived
   multiset of effect-sign families and their multiplicity. No token identity or
   energy/safety mean crosses a world boundary.
2. `M2_CONCENTRATION_ONLY`: transfer only a scalar reliability/concentration
   estimate. Its prior mean is exactly neutral and it contains no effect sign or
   token consequence.
3. `M3_ACQUISITION_POLICY_ONLY`: transfer only public-history-derived safe-probe
   policy statistics/thresholds. Current-world result prediction always starts
   empty.

For each mechanism, capped and uncapped confidence are compared as a
cross-cutting intervention, not a fourth candidate. Transfer-scratch deficit-AUC
gaps are reported at fixed interaction checkpoints. An evaluator-only correctly
aligned latent arm is a private diagnostic upper bound and is never learner
input, product state, or capability evidence.

## Required controls and gate

Every candidate runs `TRANSFER`, `SCRATCH`, `NO_TRANSFER`, `SLOW_RESET`,
`FAST_RESET`, `HISTORY_SHUFFLE`, `NO_UPDATE`, `PRIOR_CONTRADICTION`, and
`UNCAPPED_TRANSFER`, plus random, oracle and evaluator-only latent-alignment
diagnostics. Rows carry the exact public input receipt and candidate state
hashes. Private field injection, row/state/packet/source tamper and summary drift
must fail closed.

Search authorization requires, without threshold changes: mean early transfer
gain at least 5% of scratch-oracle early headroom; positive early direction in
at least 12/16 worlds; final action-conditioned effect-sign accuracy at least
0.80; the corresponding no-transfer and slow-reset controls each remove at
least half the observed gain; history shuffle materially damages learning; and
contradictory prior late loss recovers within 5% of random-oracle total
headroom. Only a passing search candidate may be frozen and consume the new
qualification once. Otherwise qualification remains unconsumed and the terminal
verdict is
`WITHIN_WORLD_LEARNING_ESTABLISHED_COMPOSITIONAL_TRANSFER_STILL_ABSENT`.

## Claim ceiling

At most this task can establish benchmark-local reuse of one explicitly named
public-history statistic across new dev-only worlds of the unchanged microworld
grammar. It cannot establish general transfer, agency, subjectivity,
consciousness, electronic life, self-generated goals or real-world survival.

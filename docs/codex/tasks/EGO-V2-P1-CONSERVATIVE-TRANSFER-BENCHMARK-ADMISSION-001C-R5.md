## Source-bank generator and admission

- it uses the common public-only navigation adapter defined below;
- it stops after complete coverage or the common cap of 128 committed primitive

Each of the twelve source runs must cover all five tokens.  The two policy runs

### Stage 2 — equal-access primitive-action acquisition

Stage 2 isolates and then crosses inference and acquisition.  Each arm starts
from the same serialized target checkpoint for its layout/world/policy pair but
generates its own history through the sole controller/engine/selector path.
No common ordered packet or forced action is injected.

Per arm and target/policy pair, caps are: 128 committed primitive actions total,

machine-dependent gate.  A checkpoint not reached by the cap is
`ACQUISITION_SUPPORT_INFEASIBLE` for that pair and cannot be hidden by averaging.

The common navigation adapter receives only the desired public token and the
arm's public observation/history.  It maintains a start-relative public map,
uses breadth-first shortest paths to an observed desired token, and otherwise
chooses the lexically first nearest unexplored frontier.  Primitive ties use the
frozen action order `turn_left,turn_right,move_forward,interact,rest`.  It emits
a unique score surface into the existing sole `survival_learning.select_action`
receipt; it may not call `transition_world`, monkeypatch an action, or add a
second action reducer.  Source generation uses the same adapter.

`COMMON_PUBLIC_BFS_NAVIGATION_V1` has the exact input object:

```text
{schema_version, desired_token, current_public_observation,
 navigation_state, committed_row_or_null}
```

The observation must have only the public schema/version and canonical 5x5
`visual`; organism, goal, run metadata, hashes, and raw history are not inputs.
`committed_row_or_null` is only the immediately preceding legal public row and
updates state before planning.  `navigation_state` has exactly
`{schema_version, relative_pose:{x,y,facing}, cells, step_count}`.  Initial pose
is `(0,0,N)`.  `cells` is a list sorted by `(y,x)` of
`{x,y,value}` where value is one of
`unknown,empty,wall,v0,v1,v2,v3,v4`; unknown cells are omitted.  On each public
observation, skip center/`self` and `occluded`, set `(dx,dy)=(column-2,row-2)`,
and rotate by facing exactly as `N:(dx,dy)`, `E:(-dy,dx)`, `S:(-dx,-dy)`,
`W:(dy,-dx)` before adding relative pose; post-observation values overwrite
prior values.  After every projection, unconditionally write the current
relative-pose cell as `empty`; this supplies the initial frontier origin and a
valid adjacent-token goal pose.  Own
committed `turned`/`moved` outcomes update pose, while blocked moves do not.

BFS node identity is `(x,y,facing)`.  Edges use the frozen primitive-action
order: turns change facing at cost 1; forward changes position at cost 1 only
into known `empty` cells.  For an observed desired-token cell, goals are known
empty adjacent cells facing it.  Choose the shortest action sequence, then the
lexicographically smallest full action sequence.  If no desired-token goal is
reachable, a frontier is a directed edge from a known empty cell into an
omitted/unknown neighbour; rank frontiers by shortest turn/move distance to the
directed edge, then `(y,x,facing_index)`, and append one `move_forward` probe.
If neither route nor frontier exists, choose `rest`.  If the desired token is
already at pre-observation `[1][2]`, choose `interact` before BFS.

`{schema_version,desired_token,selected_primitive_action,candidate_scores,
 planned_actions,navigation_state_after_observation}`.  `candidate_scores`
contains all five actions with float `1.0` only for the selected action and

with learning mode off.  The selector must return the unique 1.0 action and its
normal receipt.  No navigation cache is shared across runs or arms.
At every respawn, navigation state alone resets to initial pose, empty cells,

## Exact finite Bayesian reference

Let `P={p0,...,p4}` be the five anonymous public prototype hashes sorted by
canonical prototype bytes.  Let `Pi` be all `5! = 120` bijections from
`v0..v4` to `P`.

All 120-space arms, including structure-matched scratch, receive the same public
prototype table.  Therefore the tested transfer advantage is source **mapping-
prior** reuse, not discovery of the prototype catalogue.  This deliberately
strong scratch control is not called pure zero-source learning; the separate
target-only public lookup baseline reports that weaker comparison.

For six source mappings `s1..s6`, freeze three family priors:

- scratch: family mass `1/3`, `q_S(pi)=1/120`;
- source reuse: family mass `1/3`, `q_R(pi)=count_j[sj=pi]/6`;
- local shift: family mass `1/3`; for each source mapping assign `1/10` across
  its ten one-transposition neighbours, then average six sources, so
  `q_L(pi)=count_(j,t)[transpose_t(sj)=pi]/60`.

Duplicate mappings accumulate mass; each family normalizes to one before its
`1/3` mass.  No prior parameter is fitted on targets.

For a successful target interaction with public pre organism `x`, token `v`,
and post organism `x'`, mapping `pi` predicts
`x_hat'=round6(clamp01(x+b+z[pi(v)]))` componentwise.  The likelihood is 1 iff
all four predicted post components equal the canonical six-decimal observed
post components and 0 otherwise.  Non-interaction rows affect navigation only,
not this likelihood.  If all 120 scratch mappings have zero likelihood, the
instrument fails closed; no epsilon/noise parameter may be added after results.

Posterior update is exact normalized multiplication in rational/integer mass
where possible, with canonical sorted accumulation.  Family marginal
likelihoods and posterior family masses are reported.  The BMA predictive
distribution is the posterior mixture of deterministic realized deltas; its
point prediction is the componentwise posterior mean rounded to six decimals.

`MDL_b=-log2(Z_b)`, where `Z_b` is the sequential marginal likelihood of the
observed prefix, is an audit-equivalent prequential codelength only.  It never
re-enters posterior weights, acquisition, prediction, or the pass rule, avoiding
double-counting the same evidence.

For each query token, calculate candidate and scratch BMA point predictions.
For every candidate-posterior mapping, calculate benefit
`MAE(scratch_prediction,delta_pi)-MAE(candidate_prediction,delta_pi)` across the
four components.  The pessimistic benefit is the exact posterior-weighted lower
5% quantile (`inf{x:CDF(x)>=0.05}`).  Transfer prediction is used only when that
quantile is at least zero; otherwise prediction falls back to scratch for that
token.  This is a posterior safeguard, not a frequentist confidence interval.

Primary acquisition is exact expected information gain
`I(pi;Y_v | legal_history)` in bits over the unique mapping variable and the
finite predicted outcome partition.  Restrict to tokens with minimum successful-
query count, select maximal EIG, and break ties by `v0..v4`.  EVSI is a
separately reported challenger and has no normative role.
Posterior concentration must make EIG decay; a periodic quota is not equivalent.

These are established finite Bayesian/MDL tools, not evidence that a neural
network spontaneously learned the method.  Neural VIME/random-prior candidates
are forbidden until this reference survives the controls.

## Mandatory controls and factorial attribution

Transfer-specific controls are mandatory:

1. **structure-matched scratch Bayes:** the same 120 mappings, exact likelihood,
   BMA, EIG, budgets, and scorer, but uniform prior and no source/shift mass;
2. **consistency-set eliminator:** eliminate inconsistent exact source mappings;
   if one remains use it, if multiple remain average them, and if none remain
   fall back to structure-matched scratch; it has no three-family BMA or MDL,
   while its EIG cell uses the exact consistency distribution defined above;
3. **passive fixed-order Bayes:** exact transfer inference with target rows
   acquired in `v0..v4` order;
4. **sham bank:** replace every public source mapping by its deterministic token
   rotation `v0<-v1<-v2<-v3<-v4<-v0`, preserving source count and bytes scale;
5. **matched-capacity scratch+local:** the six lexicographic anchors plus sixty
   one-swap components and exact `1/3` family masses defined above, updated by the
   same likelihood and acquired by EIG under its own posterior;
6. **unconditional hard best-source reuse:** choose the source mapping with
   minimum cumulative four-component MAE between its predicted and observed
   public post-organism across successful target rows, tie by canonical mapping
   bytes, never fall back to scratch, and use passive lexical acquisition;
7. **uniform source averaging:** equal-entry-weight prediction over all six
   source mappings, ignore target evidence for weights, and use passive lexical
   acquisition.

The preregistered attribution panel is the full `3 x 3` cross of inference
`{transfer-reference, structure-matched-scratch, consistency-eliminator}` with
acquisition `{EIG, passive-lexical, uniform-random}`.  This is run before any
standalone challenger.  Additional equal-access acquisition challengers are
current fixed quota, deterministic round robin, D-optimal public design, exact
visit-count greedy, public-front-token heuristic, and constrained posterior
sampling.  Prediction/navigation challengers include exact visible-token lookup,
count/transition/successor tables, FSM planner, and episodic/nearest-neighbour
traversal.  A cheap equal-access control satisfying both sides blocks special

An acquisition decision occurs once at arm start and again only after a
successful desired-token interaction; the desired token is latched across all
intervening primitive actions and life resets.  Successful-query counts persist
across lives.  `A_PASSIVE` selects the lexical token with minimum count.
`A_UNIFORM` sorts that same minimum-count set and chooses index
`floor(u*set_size)` using exactly one draw per acquisition decision.  Thus it is
without replacement until all five counts tie and with balanced replacement
thereafter.  The draw cursor starts at 0 for each arm run and increments only on
an actual stochastic acquisition decision; retries consume no draw.

`A_POSTERIOR_SAMPLING` uses one draw per acquisition decision to inverse-CDF
sample one mapping in normative mapping order from the inference method's
current mapping distribution.  Among minimum-count tokens it selects the token
whose sampled four-vector has greatest MAE from the current posterior-mean
four-vector, ties lexical.  Its cursor has the same per-arm reset/retry rule.

Uniform-random and posterior-sampling controls use the same counter-based,
control-local construction with distinct registered `control_id`.  Draw index
range is every integer `0 <= i <= 511`; each draw is the first unsigned 64 bits,
big-endian, of
`SHA256(UTF8(task_id + "\x1f" + stage_id + "\x1f" + control_id + "\x1f" + i))`;
`u=(integer+0.5)/2^64`.  It contains no world, policy, checkpoint, source,
target-class, outcome, or private hash.  Draw indices and values are receipts.
Deterministic candidate values and ties use frozen lexical order; policy seeds
never enter the reference learner or its RNG.  The legacy fixed-quota control
retains its historical seed semantics and is reported separately.

## Candidate-free endpoint and joint decision rule

The primary metric at feedback budget 2 is equal-weight MAE over 5 tokens x 4
realized-delta components.  At Stage 2 it is computed separately for every
target/policy pair.  Means are descriptive only and cannot rescue a failed pair.

The effect unit is frozen before target execution from immutable environment
mechanism increments, not target mappings or observed results: compute the ten
pairwise four-component MAEs among the five `CAUSE_DELTAS` vectors and take their
median.  The sorted values are
`0.070,0.075,0.075,0.080,0.085,0.090,0.100,0.115,0.115,0.125`, hence
`E=0.0875`.  The common interaction-energy base cancels pairwise.

- true-analogy benefit threshold: `E/4 = 0.021875` absolute MAE;
- per-mismatch non-inferiority margin: `E/20 = 0.004375` absolute MAE.

The fractions `1/4` and `1/20` are preregistered substantive value choices, not
power-derived confidence bounds: the first demands recovery of one quarter of a
typical mechanism separation, while the second tolerates only five percent of
that separation as mismatch harm.  They may be judged too strict or too lenient
after this frozen experiment, but they may not be changed to rescue it.

Stage 1 passes the effect rule only if target 81 improves over
structure-matched scratch by at least `0.021875` and every target 76..80 has
regret at most `0.004375`.  Stage 2 requires the same inequalities separately
for both policy seeds: both target-81 pairs pass and all ten mismatch pairs pass.
This logical intersection is the simultaneous joint rule; no p-value or
credible interval is inferred from two seeds.

Deleting source and source-derived shift mass must reduce true-analogy benefit
by at least `0.021875` and make that rule fail.  Deleting EIG must change at
least one non-tie query before budget 2 **and** worsen target-81 budget-2 MAE by
at least `0.004375` or increase the worst mismatch regret by at least
`0.004375`; a tie-only change is insufficient.  No equal-access control may
satisfy the joint two-sided rule.

The EIG/information-value deletion requirement is **Stage 2 only**.  Stage 1 has
no acquisition claim and is adjudicated only on fixed-packet inference.

Budget 2 is mechanistically fixed: after two distinct token observations, three
of five assignments remain unresolved, so source similarity can still help or
harm and rapid rejection is testable.  Budgets `0,1,4,5` in Stage 1 and
`0,1,4,8` in Stage 2 are diagnostic curves, not alternate pass checkpoints.

## Exact L1 decision and acquisition

### Weighted-median decision

For values `x` with nonnegative integer-equivalent weights `w` and total `W>0`:

```text
lower = min{x : 2*sum_{v<=x} w(v) >= W}
upper = max{x : 2*sum_{v>=x} w(v) >= W}
midpoint_fraction = Fraction(lower+upper, 2)
midpoint_integer = round_half_even(midpoint_fraction)
```

The producer must assert `lower <= midpoint_integer <= upper` and that lower, midpoint integer, and upper have identical minimal weighted L1 risk. The normative `D_L1_MEDIAN` uses the midpoint integer componentwise. `D_SCRATCH_L1` uses the same rule on same-history scratch. Observed tokens equal their public prototype exactly; posterior means are diagnostic-forbidden, not alternative decisions.

For `D_LCB05_FALLBACK`, at each unqueried token compute transfer action `a_T`, same-history scratch action `a_S`, and for every mapping `pi` in the transfer posterior:

```text
b(pi) = L1(a_S, P[pi(token)]) - L1(a_T, P[pi(token)]).
```

Sort benefits ascending and choose the smallest `b` whose cumulative posterior weight satisfies `20*cumulative_weight >= total_weight`. Use transfer iff that exact lower-5% quantile is nonnegative; otherwise use `a_S`. This decision is recomputed inside every hypothetical H2 branch used by acquisition.

Run the complete instrument three times with the global median representative set to lower, midpoint integer, and upper. If the eventual non-sensitivity verdict differs, dispatch `ACTIVE_TRANSFER_MEDIAN_SELECTION_SENSITIVE`; no positive verdict is allowed.

### Loss-matched one-step acquisition

At H1, scale the arm's effective posterior to primitive nonnegative integers `W_H1(pi)`. For eligible query `q` and each outcome `y` with positive mass, append hypothetical H2 `(q,y)`, compute that arm's registered prediction `a_qy`, and define:

```text
R(q) = sum_y sum_{pi with W_H1(pi)>0 and pi[q]=y} W_H1(pi)
             * sum_{r not in {initial_token,q}} L1(a_qy[r], P[pi[r]]).
```

The inner endpoint is exactly the three still-unqueried tokens and 12 components. No observed-token zeroing appears in `R(q)`. `A_L1_EVSI` minimizes this exact integer. Its posterior-predictive outcome weighting and one-query horizon are fully contained in the formula; no future or evaluator truth enters it.

For `A_EIG`, let `N_qy=sum_pi W_H1(pi)*1[pi[q]=y]`. Deterministic observation makes mutual information equal outcome entropy. With equal total weight across `q`, exact maximization is exact minimization of

```text
E(q) = product over y with N_qy>0 of N_qy ** N_qy.
```

`A_MAX_OUTCOME_ENTROPY` is a separate callable implementation over posterior-predictive outcome groups and must reproduce every `E(q)`, minimizing set, and lexical choice byte-for-byte. Floating entropy cannot affect a verdict.

Other acquisitions are exact: `A_PASSIVE` chooses the smallest eligible token; `A_FIXED_V1..V4` choose their named token in base runs; `A_NO_QUERY` spends no second row; `A_UNIFORM_MIXTURE` is the exact 1/4 expectation of the four fixed branches and cannot rescue any trajectory gate. `A_ALL_QUERY_ORACLE` is verifier-only enumeration of all four legal truth-blind second queries; its witnesses never enter an arm or verdict.

For every bank and every gate-bearing acquisition, enumerate every deterministic H1 policy formed by selecting one member of each exact minimizing set (at most `4^5` policies per arm/bank). Pairwise gates enumerate the Cartesian product of the two compared policy sets. The lexical policy is normative. If any alternative changes the eventual non-sensitivity verdict, dispatch `ACTIVE_TRANSFER_QUERY_TIE_SENSITIVE`; no positive verdict is allowed.

## Exact endpoints and decomposition

For one-query trajectory arms report normalized MAE as raw absolute micro-unit loss divided by `1,000,000` and:

1. `full_endpoint_mae`: all five tokens x four components, denominator 20;
2. `own_unqueried_forward_mae`: exactly the three tokens not queried by that arm, denominator 12;
3. `common_unqueried_forward_mae`: tokens queried by neither candidate nor comparator, denominator 12 when second queries match and 8 otherwise;
4. `same_history_forward_mae`: compare candidate action to `D_SCRATCH_L1` on the identical candidate H2 and its same three unqueried tokens, denominator 12;
5. queried/asymmetric contribution and common-forward contribution as exact raw totals.

If candidate query `qC` and comparator query `qB` differ, define:

```text
common_raw = sum loss_B - loss_C over tokens outside {initial,qC,qB}
query_asymmetry_raw = loss_B(qC) - loss_C(qB)
full_improvement_raw = common_raw + query_asymmetry_raw
```

When queries match, `query_asymmetry_raw=0` and common covers the three unqueried tokens. The producer asserts this identity for every row. Directly queried-token loss cannot count as forward gain.

No-query arms have one observed token, four own-unqueried tokens, and denominator 16. Uniform-mixture metrics are exact expectations of the four fixed branches; they have no single prediction or own/common token set and cannot satisfy or rescue a pointwise gate. The all-query oracle is diagnostic only.

All 120 truths run for every unique bank. Distinct exact-member mappings are equal-weight primary units; entry-weighted member summaries are diagnostic only. Means cannot hide mapping-level failure.

## Frozen gates

All normalized thresholds are exact rationals. Improvement means comparator MAE minus candidate MAE; regret means candidate MAE minus comparator MAE.

For each of the 75 primary roles, define the canonical member before execution as its lexicographically smallest distinct source mapping. A conservative or raw candidate clears the **member-and-forward gate** only if, versus `PUBLIC_L1_RISK_DP`:

1. every distinct exact member has full-endpoint improvement at least `0.021875`;
2. every distinct exact member has common-unqueried and same-history forward improvement at least `0`;
3. the canonical member has both common-unqueried and same-history forward improvement at least `0.004375`.

Thus queried-token-only improvement cannot pass.

Safety is two separate predicates over every D2--D5 target of every primary role:

- **strict safety:** full-endpoint regret versus `PUBLIC_L1_RISK_DP` is at most `0` for every point;
- **bounded safety:** regret is at most `0.004375` for every point. Bounded-only is never described as not worse than scratch.

The joint ablation gate additionally requires:

- on `B_SEPARABLE`, active deletion changes a unique query and the candidate beats its passive deletion by at least `0.004375` on common-unqueried MAE for at least one exact member;
- source deletion makes the member-and-forward gate fail and reduces the full benefit of **every distinct exact member in every primary role** by at least `0.021875`;
- the local-deletion and all gate-bearing causal-effect predicates above pass;
- on `MULT_6`, ordinary transfer and multiplicity-flattened transfer have byte-identical semantic states, query scores, choices, and predictions; six duplicates cannot manufacture multiplicity evidence beyond the equal-mass singleton support;
- `B_COLLISION` emits its unavoidable leaf witness and never reports full source-support identification;
- on the witnessing `B_BALANCED_MARGINAL` H1, `ARM_I_CONSISTENCY__A_EIG__D_L1_MEDIAN` has all four tokens as exact minimizers and the `I_CONSISTENCY` maximum-outcome-entropy alias matches;
- on `B_DECOY`, the two frozen unique minimizers exist and differ.

For a candidate `C`, a registered trajectory control `K` Pareto-matches/dominates when, over every primary-role target, `K` has no larger full MAE than `C` and, over every distinct exact member, `K` has no larger common-unqueried MAE, with at least one strict improvement or byte-identical metrics everywhere. The invalidating control set is:

- all seven `I_CONSISTENCY` core arms;
- all seven `I_SCRATCH` core arms;
- the five transfer LCB controls using `A_PASSIVE` or `A_FIXED_V1..V4`.

Additionally, to establish positive non-equivalence, the candidate must beat `BANK_CONSISTENCY_L1_RISK_DP` on at least one exact member by `0.004375` common-unqueried MAE while being no worse by any positive amount on full MAE for any primary target. A control match or failure of this explicit consistency Pareto rule sets `control_match=true`.

The raw-upside predicate is true when the raw challenger clears the member-and-forward gate. The conservative **bounded core** is member-and-forward plus bounded safety; its **strict core** substitutes strict safety. The **attribution gate** is all gate-bearing ablations/property checks, replay/leakage prerequisites, and no omitted execution. `bounded_joint = bounded_core and attribution_gate`; `strict_joint = strict_core and attribution_gate`. Non-equivalence to the structural public/consistency/fixed controls is evaluated after joint clearance. Expected equality to `CANDIDATE_RULE_AMORTIZED_LOOKUP` is reported separately and caps the result at algorithmic-reference evidence.

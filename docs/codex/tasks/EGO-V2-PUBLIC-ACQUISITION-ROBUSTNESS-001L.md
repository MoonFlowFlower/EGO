# EGO-V2-PUBLIC-ACQUISITION-ROBUSTNESS-001L

## Purpose and protected evidence

Explain and adjudicate the packet variance between frozen 001K qualification
recovery `54.29%` and replication recovery `40.95%` without rerunning either
packet. Use only new, precommitted dev-only assignments. Original 001J heldout
and all 001J/001K sources, results, rows, and artifacts are read-only.

## Frozen budget

- one successor campaign only;
- the unchanged 001K candidate is a carry-over control and does not count as a
  new substantive candidate;
- at most two substantive successor candidates: a stateful harmful-front
  escape and a stateless unseen-frontier priority rule;
- repeatable search-dev diagnosis only before candidate freeze;
- one single-use qualification and one single-use replication;
- no tuning after qualification reveal and no candidate change for replication.

## Mechanism hypotheses

1. **Denominator/packet composition variance:** oracle-random headroom varies
   with topology, mapping, and initial dominant deficit. Strongest rebuttal:
   public loss itself, not only the denominator, worsens on the same slices.
2. **Unsafe unknown-token acquisition:** the bounded information bonus is not
   conditioned strongly enough on the dominant deficit/risk margin. Strongest
   rebuttal: geometry/path reachability, not probing, may dominate failures.
3. **Navigation reacquisition variance:** public egocentric target selection can
   repeatedly lose useful targets in some geometry/mapping combinations.
   Strongest rebuttal: 001K target retention was weaker on its search packet.

The cheapest discriminator is row-level slice decomposition of stored 001K
outputs followed by one new search-dev packet on the frozen candidate. Only one
mechanism variable may be changed for the second candidate.

Search selection is preregistered: highest unique-world mean recovery, then
positive-world count, then earlier first beneficial interaction, then shorter
harmful-front loop. A successor must improve recovery and its targeted
diagnostic relative to the carry-over control; otherwise formal packets are not
consumed.

## Unchanged gates

Qualification and replication must each have recovery `>= 0.50`, at least
`12/16` positive worlds, positive public gain, effect-sign accuracy `>= 0.80`,
material no-update/feedback-shuffle/posterior-ablation damage, exact replay,
independent row recomputation, clean legal inputs, and fail-closed leakage and
tamper positive controls. Both formal packets must pass before authorizing the
minimal two-timescale learner.

If either fails, verdict is `ROBUSTNESS_NOT_ESTABLISHED_M1_NOT_AUTHORIZED`; the
campaign stops and the product lane retains only the bounded Bayesian/reference
mode. Thresholds must not be lowered.

## Claim ceiling

Passing establishes robustness of public rule acquisition only within new
dev-only packets of the same frozen grammar. It does not establish general
transfer, agency, consciousness, electronic life, or real-world survival.

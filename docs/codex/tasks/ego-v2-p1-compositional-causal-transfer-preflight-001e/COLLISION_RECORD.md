# Collision record — EGO-V2-P1-COMPOSITIONAL-CAUSAL-TRANSFER-PREFLIGHT-001E

## Candidate 1 — enlarge or retune the permutation grammar

- **Mechanism:** increase token count, retune the conservative gate, or train a
  selector on the same source-mapping problem.
- **Evidence it could produce:** larger apparent headroom and a different
  reuse/fallback frontier.
- **Strongest cheap match:** consistency/risk DP and a complete amortized public-
  state lookup.
- **Hard-coding/leakage risk:** task-ID/bank memorization, threshold tuning, and
  a neural table disguised as inference.
- **Smallest falsifier:** a train-only lookup or independent structural control
  matches on the finite public domain.
- **Expected failure:** exact-member gain still trades against nonmember tails.
- **Strongest rebuttal:** increasing `n!` does not create a new causal program or
  new identifying evidence.
- **Disposition:** reject after R6/R7/001D/X1; do not begin a third repair round.

## Candidate 2 — sequential changepoint/regret control now

- **Mechanism:** deploy old knowledge until a prediction error, detect a change,
  reset/localize, and minimize cumulative online regret.
- **Evidence it could produce:** contradiction-driven recovery and longer-horizon
  behavioral benefit.
- **Strongest cheap match:** a hand-written reset threshold, periodic probe, or
  count/change table can explain recovery.
- **Hard-coding/leakage risk:** the change time, horizon, reset policy, and probe
  cost can encode the desired behavior.
- **Smallest falsifier:** a fixed reset/change detector matches under equal
  access or the result disappears under horizon/probe-cost controls.
- **Expected failure:** the estimand becomes online control rather than transfer
  headroom, so cause attribution is ambiguous.
- **Strongest rebuttal:** this is operationally close to the user's final goal,
  but it combines too many unknowns before a transferable reference exists.
- **Disposition:** defer to a separate successor only if the compositional
  reference is admitted.

## Candidate 3 — selected exact Boolean compositional reference

- **Mechanism:** a seven-term degree-two Boolean causal program, sparse scratch
  prior, exact-source spike, six one-rule local shifts, BMA, and one exact Brier-
  risk intervention.
- **Evidence it could produce:** source/local forward prediction, bounded
  nonlocal risk, active-query value, and attribution through deletion.
- **Strongest cheap match:** scratch-active Bayes, source consistency,
  nearest/Hamming program, hard-family MDL, or graph/history lookup.
- **Hard-coding/leakage risk:** coefficient/program literals, evaluator class,
  future outputs, a precomputed eight-row table, favourable-source selection, or
  a candidate-rule lookup misreported as learning.
- **Smallest falsifier:** complete `128*128` enumeration; any joint gate failure;
  any non-tautological control Pareto-match; or tie/leakage/replay failure.
- **Expected failure:** exact reuse helps but local or nonlocal risk fails, active
  query collapses to the best fixed query, or a simpler control matches.
- **Strongest rebuttal:** one source and a finite 128-program grammar cannot
  establish meta-learning or neural non-memorization.
- **Disposition:** select only as a low-cost algorithmic admission before a
  larger variable-grammar learned-candidate task.

permutation surface.  It uses complete exact enumeration and no RNG, optimizer,
world, or candidate.  Candidate 2 remains the likely later operational reframe,
but only after the reference question survives.

## Program goal

Produce the smallest runnable, observable and interactive survival-learning
vertical slice in the existing V2 runtime while running one bounded robustness
adjudication. Product availability and scientific authorization are separate:
the Bayesian reference may ship default-off even if M1 remains unauthorized.

## Non-goals

No LLM, network, autostart, background dispatch, new controller/store/replay,
world grammar change, heldout consumption, `joi-demo` edits, large neural
learner, consciousness/agency/electronic-life claim, push, or tag.

## Acceptance

- public action-effect learning and real post-transition update;
- deficit/risk/uncertainty-sensitive action selection;
- persistence across death with explicit reset semantics;
- public-state visualization and direct command;
- controls for update, shuffle, posterior, slow/fast reset, scratch and random;
- transfer and wrong-prior recovery measured on dev-only worlds;
- exact replay plus independent row/leakage/tamper verification;
- R campaign obeys its two-candidate and two-formal-packet stop budget.

## Exact public program grammar

The public input is one three-bit composition:

```text
x=(x1,x2,x3) in {0,1}^3
```

Program coefficients use frozen order:

```text
beta=(b0,b1,b2,b3,b12,b13,b23) in {0,1}^7
```

The deterministic public outcome is:

```text
h_beta(x) = b0 XOR (b1 AND x1) XOR (b2 AND x2)
             XOR (b3 AND x3) XOR (b12 AND x1 AND x2)
             XOR (b13 AND x1 AND x3) XOR (b23 AND x2 AND x3)
```

All 128 programs are enumerated by binary integers `0..127`, most-significant
bit first in the coefficient order above.  Compositions are enumerated by binary
integers `0..7`, most-significant bit first in `(x1,x2,x3)` order.  Source truth
tables contain all eight rows in that order.  Learners receive the table, not
coefficients or a program ID.  Program execution traces must record the seven
term contributions and cumulative XOR so replay cannot be replaced by an
unexplained eight-row literal.

### Sparse scratch prior

`b0 ~ Bernoulli(1/2)`.  Each of the six other coefficients is independently
`Bernoulli(1/4)`.  If `k(h)` is the number of active non-intercept coefficients:

```text
p0(h) = 3^(6-k(h)) / 8192
```

The producer must derive and verify `sum_h p0(h)=1` exactly.

Given source program `hs`, the three family priors are:

- scratch `q_S(h)=p0(h)`;
- exact reuse `q_E(h)=1[h=hs]`;
- local shift `q_L(h)=1/6` iff `h` differs from `hs` in exactly one of the six
  non-intercept coefficients and has the same intercept.

Family masses are `1/3` each.  With common denominator `73728`, the exact
unconditional program weight is:

```text
w(h|hs) = 3*3^(6-k(h))
          + 24576*1[h=hs]
          + 4096*1[h in N1(hs)]
```

No smoothing, fitted prior, float, RNG, MDL penalty, or target label may alter
this weight.

## Legal history, posterior, prediction, and intervention

Every case receives the complete anonymous source table and the fixed first
target row `x=000`.  It may choose exactly one distinct query from
`001,010,011,100,101,110,111`.  After observing that public bit, it predicts the
six remaining unqueried outcomes.

For history `H`, deterministic likelihood is:

```text
L_h(H) = product_i 1[h(x_i)=y_i]
```

Posterior program mass is proportional to `w(h|hs)*L_h(H)`.  Family marginal
likelihoods and posterior family masses are independently reported.  BMA
predictive probability is the exact posterior fraction of compatible weight
predicting one.  Under Brier loss its Bayes action is that probability.

For legal candidate query `q` and hypothetical outcome `y`, recompute the exact
posterior and terminal predictive probabilities for the six remaining
compositions.  The acquisition score is exact posterior expected total Brier
risk:

```text
R(q|H) = sum_y P(y|q,H) *
         sum_{x not in H union {q}} p_x^(q,y)*(1-p_x^(q,y))
```

Minimize `R` exactly with `Fraction`; lexical order is normative among exact
ties.  For tie sensitivity, compute the lexical primary plus the pointwise
minimum-loss and pointwise maximum-loss endpoint over every exact minimizing
query for every source/target case.  Because every reported aggregate is a
nonnegative weighted sum of case losses, these two envelopes bound every global
deterministic tie policy without enumerating a Cartesian policy space.  If the
final verdict differs across the lexical, optimistic-envelope, or pessimistic-
envelope reductions, dispatch `QUERY_TIE_SENSITIVE` and forbid a positive
result.

`MDL_F(H)=-log2((1/3)*m_F(H))` is an audit codelength only.  The independent
hard-family comparator selects the greatest exact family marginal and then uses
that family's own posterior predictive.  MDL must never re-enter BMA weights,
query scores, predictions, or gates.

## Complete finite evaluation population

Enumerate every `(source_program,target_program)` pair in lexical program order:

```text
128 * 128 = 16,384 cases per trajectory arm
```

Evaluator strata are derived outside learner bytes:

- `EXACT`: `target=source`;
- `LOCAL`: same intercept and non-intercept Hamming distance exactly one;
- `NONLOCAL`: every other target.

For aggregate risk, source programs have uniform mass `1/128`.  Within each
source, exact has its one target; local is uniform across six neighbours; and
nonlocal target weights are the exact scratch prior `p0(target)` conditioned on
the nonlocal set.  The producer must prove every conditional denominator is
positive and normalized.  Nominal component weights are
`NONLOCAL:1/3,EXACT:1/3,LOCAL:1/3`; scratch-heavy weights are
`NONLOCAL:1/2,EXACT:1/4,LOCAL:1/4`.

Endpoint loss is the arithmetic mean of exact Brier loss over the six common
unqueried compositions after the second row.  Each component risk is the exact
weighted mean over its frozen population.  Relative improvement is
`(baseline-candidate)/baseline`; a zero baseline denominator makes the
instrument invalid rather than defining a favourable ratio.

## Frozen admission gates

All thresholds are project-specific prospective value choices, not textbook
constants or confidence bounds.  A future implementation may return a negative
result but may not tune them.

1. **Completeness:** all 16,384 source-target pairs for every registered arm;
   exact priors/weights/strata/queries; no sampling.
2. **Nominal gain:** candidate nominal risk relative improvement over scratch
   active is at least `1/20`.
3. **Non-compensating transfer:** exact and local component relative
   improvements are each at least `1/20`.
4. **Nonlocal safety:** candidate nonlocal relative regret versus scratch active
   is at most `1/20`.
5. **Scratch-heavy value:** candidate scratch-heavy relative improvement is at
   least `1/100`.
6. **Active necessity:** candidate nominal risk relative improvement over the
   globally best fixed-query control is at least `1/100`; at least one legal H1
   state has a unique non-lexical query and positive common-unqueried endpoint
   gain.
7. **Ablation sensitivity:** source deletion removes the exact and nominal gain;
   local deletion makes the local rule fail; feedback mask makes the active
   endpoint rule fail; active deletion makes the active-necessity rule fail.
   Uniform-scratch replacement is diagnostic and cannot rescue.
8. **Control immunity:** scratch active, source consistency, hard-family,
   nearest/Hamming, or any legal graph/cache/history control may not match or
   dominate the candidate simultaneously on nominal gain, exact/local gain,
   nonlocal safety, and scratch-heavy value.
9. **Replay/leakage:** fresh replay and independent recomputation agree; source
   and target coefficients, relation/stratum, future outcomes, scorer, IDs,
   paths, hashes, and encoded aliases never enter an arm; every planted positive
   control is rejected.
10. **Tail disclosure:** report maximum nonlocal regret, nonlocal negative-
    transfer weighted mass, and worst-10%-mass nonlocal CVaR.  These disclosures
    cannot rescue or alone fail the bounded aggregate gate.

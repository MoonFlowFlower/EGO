The question is whether legal public histories contain enough information for
an exact Bayesian reference to identify the current anonymous-token latent
alignment at lower survival cost than scratch. The candidate may know the
public frozen grammar family and all 5! legal mappings, but not the realized
mapping, world/layout ID, seed, private pose, oracle action, packet label or
future information.

## Competing mechanisms to distinguish

1. **Publicly identifiable and economic.** Direct interactions eliminate
   mapping hypotheses quickly enough that information-aware planning gains
   early deficit AUC. Strongest rebuttal: the canonical within-world posterior
   already gets the useful feedback, so exact joint marginalization may not
   improve behavior. Cheapest discriminator: exhaustive posterior filtering
   plus one frozen exact-reference search/replication comparison.
2. **Intervention-identifiable but uneconomic.** Public action/feedback can
   identify the actionable alignment, but the required navigation and probe
   deficits exceed the remaining value of the information. Strongest rebuttal:
   a risk-constrained Bayes-adaptive policy could select only probes with
   positive posterior value. Cheapest discriminator: compare symbolic
   interaction lower bounds, actual first-reliable-alignment cost, and the
   evaluator-only aligned upper bound.
3. **Structurally non-identifiable.** Two or more named latent mappings induce
   the same public distribution under the frozen grammar. Strongest rebuttal:
   cause-specific respawn geometry might eventually reveal the label. Cheapest
   discriminator: enumerate equivalence classes under the public transition
   likelihood while marginalizing exchangeable nuisance generation; then
   distinguish passive, intervention-only and permanent equivalence.

## Exact-reference boundary

The single allowed candidate keeps a normalized posterior over every legal
mapping still consistent with public history. It never selects the evaluator's
true mapping. Each action diagnostic reports posterior-expected deficit,
terminal risk, information gain and survival cost. A private-aligned arm is
evaluator-owned and excluded from all public capability gates.

Rows must expose enough public receipts for an independent verifier to rebuild
the posterior, entropy, equivalent-mapping count, exact and behavioral Bayes
errors, cumulative deficit AUC, and trace chain. Leakage and state/row/packet
tamper positive controls must fail closed.

## Decision rule

Run the symbolic/small enumeration audit first. Run the one frozen exact
reference only if the audit finds at least intervention-identifiable actionable
classes. A learner implementation gap requires, on both frozen dev-only packets:

- exact-reference early deficit-AUC gain over scratch is positive;
- recovery is at least 5% of scratch to private-aligned early headroom;
- at least 12/16 worlds have positive direction;
- feedback shuffle, no-update/posterior ablation, or information-gain ablation
  materially damages the gain;
- independent row recomputation, leakage and tamper checks all pass.

Otherwise the terminal verdict is
`PUBLIC_LATENT_ALIGNMENT_NOT_IDENTIFIABLE_OR_NOT_ECONOMIC_UNDER_CURRENT_GRAMMAR`
and compositional-transfer pursuit stops on the current benchmark.

## Claim ceiling

At most this task can establish benchmark-local public identifiability and
economic acquisition of an anonymous-token alignment within the frozen
microworld grammar. It cannot establish general transfer, agency, subjectivity,
consciousness, electronic life, self-generated goals, or real-world survival.

## 3. Bounded audit

### 3.1 Hypothesis

If a candidate-independent task distribution contains residual
action-conditioned outcome structure that is not directly decodable from the
current observation or legal full history, then one fixed online-updating
predictor may improve held-out outcome calibration and bounded legal-action
ranking within a preregistered few-shot horizon relative to no-update,
lookup/decoder and candidate-rule-amortized controls.

### 3.2 Strongest baseline explanation

The strongest baseline explanation is that all apparent adaptation can be
matched by one or more same-access shortcuts:

- full-history observation decoder;
- exact lookup or nearest-neighbor retrieval;
- transition/count/successor table;
- drift-aware regime inference with continual replay;
- the candidate's own update rule evaluated offline or batch-amortized;
- a genuinely fitted recurrent/meta learner;
- a direct task-objective optimizer;
- per-task specialist policies.

A fair control matching the candidate means there is no evidence that online
learned updating supplied unique product headroom.

A specialist rival winning on its own narrow task does not by itself falsify a
breadth claim. Control equivalence does.

### 3.3 Strongest reason the framing may be invalid

The route may be invalid because:

- “learned” could be a renamed lookup or transition table;
- task/family/split/fixture identity could leak the outcome rule;
- legal observation history could already determine the target;
- the candidate could author or influence ground truth;
- parameter changes could occur while the selector ignores predictions;
- the predictor could be decorative beside a second hard-coded selector;
- stored-action replay could be mistaken for causal recomputation;
- the proposed environment could have no fair legal-observation headroom;
- implementation could be a semantic rename of old K0-R.

### 3.4 Framing falsifiers

The learning/adaptation framing is falsified for the frozen distribution if any
of the following occurs:

- candidate-independent preflight finds no fair headroom;
- observation-only or full-history decoding reaches the ceiling band;
- any control baseline ties or beats the candidate within the equivalence band;
- the candidate's own batch/offline update rule matches online performance;
- no-update or shuffled-feedback intervention does not collapse the claimed
  held-out advantage by the preregistered amount;
- predictor bypass leaves ranking and decision quality unchanged;
- checkpoint/history swap does not alter predictions where it should;
- held-out novelty or forward-transfer advantage is absent;
- replay does not consume serialized state and legal observation to recompute;
- a planted leakage positive control is not caught.

### 3.5 Evidence that remains insufficient

- green unit tests;

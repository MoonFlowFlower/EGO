## 4. Parent-card semantic clarifications

### 4.1 Target determinability

For a stochastic outcome, target determinability means statistical
identifiability of:

```text
P(outcome | legal observation, legal history, queried legal action)
```

or expected utility under the legal channel.

It does not mean the realized random outcome must be deterministically
recoverable.

Private latent state may generate outcomes but cannot enter the legal packet,
legal oracle or admission reference.

### 4.2 Baseline roles

Panel membership and claim effect are separate.

Invalidating controls include trivial predictors, passive decoders, lookup,
cache and no-feedback explanations. If these saturate legal headroom, the
surface is rejected.

Genuine fitted learners, direct adaptive estimators and per-task specialists
are capability rivals. Their matching performance may remove novelty or
superiority claims, but does not convert genuine legal-channel adaptation into
non-learning by definition.

Candidate-specific controls such as the future candidate's own batched rule,
no-update version and internal ablations remain mandatory for a future
candidate task. They cannot run here because `candidate_present=false`.

The machine-readable role registry in `PREFLIGHT_CONTRACT.json` is binding.

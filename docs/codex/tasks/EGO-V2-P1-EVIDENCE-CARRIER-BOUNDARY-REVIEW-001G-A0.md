  latency boundary and proved optimized/scalar byte equality, but its frozen
  verdict correctly remained `BLOCKED_BOUNDARY_OR_REPLAY_REGRESSION` because
  world 54's raw canonical-JSON trace mean was `32847.16949152543B`, or
  `79.16949152543B` above `32768B`. R3 and 001F now constitute two failed rounds
  on this performance/carrier framing. Continuing to shave bytes would optimize
  the observed test, while simply waiving the threshold would rewrite negative
  evidence. The unresolved question is whether raw per-row JSON size is a valid
  prerequisite for testing the next learning unknown or a legacy engineering

- **Prior negative evidence:** preserve R1/R2/R3/R4 and all 001F source,
  artifacts, rows, thresholds, and verdict. 001F remains failed permanently; no
  successor may relabel or rerun it into a pass.
- **Layer:** engineering-governance and measurement-instrument review;
  `science_weight=0`.
- **Current stage:** `PREDICTIVE_REPLAY_LATENCY_REPAIRED`,
  `RAW_JSON_TRACE_MEAN_BOUNDARY_FAILED`,
  `CURRENT_OUTCOME_CONDITIONED_ADDITIVE_FRAMING_EXHAUSTED`.
- **Hypothesis:** a two-axis contract can preserve non-negotiable semantic
  replay/tamper/recompute evidence while deriving any carrier-size budget from
  an independently stated consumer/resource constraint rather than from the

## Collision-before-collapse record

### Candidate 1 — keep `32768B` as a universal mechanism prerequisite

- **Evidence:** strict continuity with prior gates.
- **Strongest cheap match:** any arbitrary round-number storage limit.
- **Risk:** blocks the learning unknown for an 0.24% carrier miss even when all
  replay latency, exactness, tamper, database, and row-readback gates pass.
- **Smallest falsifier:** locate a real UI, SQLite, export, memory, or latency
  consumer that fails above 32768 mean bytes.
- **Expected failure:** no causal connection to mechanism validity is found.

### Candidate 2 — waive or raise the limit after seeing 001F

- **Evidence:** immediate development eligibility.
- **Strongest cheap match:** post-hoc threshold tuning.
- **Risk:** invalidates preregistration discipline and teaches the process to
  erase negative evidence.
- **Smallest falsifier:** the proposed replacement number is derived from the
  observed `32847.17B` rather than an external resource requirement.
- **Expected failure:** an unjustified cosmetic pass.
- **Decision:** rejected.

### Candidate 3 — separate semantic evidence from carrier engineering (selected
for audit only)

- **Evidence:** one immutable semantic gate (full fresh recomputation, exact
  scalar/optimized behavior, tamper rejection, row readback) plus one separately
  derived storage/observability envelope.
- **Strongest cheap match:** deleting evidence fields and retaining only hashes.
- **Risk:** a compact carrier can become lossy, unauditable, or UI-invisible.
- **Smallest falsifier:** fail to reconstruct and authenticate every compacted
  field needed by the frozen analysis and UI, or fail a real consumer budget.
- **Expected failure:** no independent resource constraint exists, leaving size
  as a convenience metric rather than an admission gate.
- **Decision:** selected only for read-only instrument audit; no new boundary is
  authorized by this card.

# Collision Record — Card 2 ITL Authority Sync

## Real objective

Make Ego a fail-closed consumer of the committed ITL Card 2 bank-admission
route, not an author of a parallel route, while preserving execution=false and
the science firewall.

## Candidate A — edit Ego's bank-action prose (rejected)

- Strongest baseline: a readable statement appears aligned.
- Smallest falsifier: omit a lineage or expand mutation paths; no callable
  source/diff binding detects it.
- Failure: prose becomes another route authority and does not close the four
  committed ITL dependencies.

## Candidate B — keep Ego's generic binding and add the ITL binding (rejected)

- Strongest baseline: both actions appear to lead to Card 2 banking.
- Smallest falsifier: consume whichever action has the broader path allowance.
- Failure: dual live actions and a permanent second control path.

## Candidate C — exact Git-object transcription plus computed local gates (selected)

- ITL route state and Phase-B review receipt are read from committed objects.
- Every leaf is crosswalked to an exact Ego transcription and compared by a
  callable validator.
- Effective action availability is computed from the ITL dependency list plus
  actual path, lineage, and committed-review gates.
- Ego's old generic binding becomes historical-only rather than co-authority.

## Strongest invalidation

This framing is invalid if the committed ITL route cannot be read exactly, if
its fields cannot be transcribed without local synthesis, if review provenance
cannot be bound to the committed diff, or if lineage completeness still depends
on self-reported Program State rows.

## Evidence still insufficient

Green tests, generated-view consistency, and Claude review are insufficient for
Card 2 bank/execution, headroom, learning, mechanism, runtime, mainline,
science-successor, readiness, agency, subjectivity, or consciousness claims.

## Risk audit

- Hard-coding: source values must come from pinned ITL blobs, not verdict
  literals in tests.
- Local optimum / Zeno: this is the single required sync; a third governance
  repair stops the task.
- Evidence leakage: Card 2 science weight remains zero and cannot satisfy old
  S0X or a future science successor.
- Weak baseline: governance consistency alone is the ceiling.
- Schema split / second logic path: the generic Ego binding is removed from live
  consumption and ITL remains the sole route authority.
- Replay weakness: crosswalk, lineage, changed paths, review binding, and
  generated views are recomputed.
- Claim inflation: field sync and guard enforcement only.

## Acceptance, stop, and rollback

Acceptance, stop conditions, exact files, and rollback are defined in the Stage
Card. Any scope expansion or inability to fail closed is a STOP, not a reason to
invent a wider control plane.

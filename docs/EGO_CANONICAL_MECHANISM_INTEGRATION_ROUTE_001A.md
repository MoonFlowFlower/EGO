the old `ego_operator_first_transition` workstream was both enabled and
mainline-connected. Twenty-five PSPC-named workstreams were all disabled and
non-mainline. The result was useful isolated engineering plus negative evidence,
but no single new product loop.

The route therefore optimizes main-flow effect rather than artifact count.
